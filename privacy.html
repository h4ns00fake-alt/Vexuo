2. Information we collect

We only collect what the Bot needs to work. Depending on which features are used, this may include:

  • Discord identifiers: user IDs, server (guild) IDs, channel IDs, role IDs and message IDs that Discord provides when you interact with the Bot.
  • Public profile data: your Discord username, display name and avatar URL, only when a feature needs to show or reference them.
  • Command and interaction data: the commands, buttons, menus or forms you use, together with their options and the time they were used.
  • Server settings: configuration chosen by server administrators (for example, prefixes, enabled features, and selected channels or roles).
  • Feature data: data created by using features of the Bot, such as stored settings, counters or logs. [EDIT THIS LINE to match what Vexuo really stores, e.g. moderation logs, economy data, Roblox usernames/IDs.]
  • Message content: the Bot only processes message content when it is needed to respond to a command or to run a feature a server has turned on. We do not read or keep private messages between other users, and we do not store message content unless a specific feature (such as a log) requires it.
  • Technical logs: error reports and diagnostic information (such as timestamps and the command that failed) used to fix bugs.

We do not intentionally collect passwords, Discord login tokens, email addresses, payment details, government ID, precise location, or any other sensitive personal data. Please never share such information with the Bot.

3. How we use information

  • To provide, operate and maintain the Bot and its features.
  • To respond to your commands and remember settings chosen by server administrators.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate abuse, spam, exploits and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To fix bugs, monitor stability and improve the Bot.
  • To answer support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. We only share it in these limited cases:

  • Discord: the Bot runs on Discord, so your use of it is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Service providers: infrastructure providers that host the Bot or its database (for example, a hosting or cloud database provider). They only process data on our behalf to keep the Bot running.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the Bot or the public.

5. Data retention

  • Server settings and feature data are kept while the Bot remains in your server, so that features keep working.
  • If the Bot is removed from a server, the data tied to that server may be deleted within [30] days, unless we need to keep it for security or legal reasons.
  • Technical and error logs are kept for a short period (typically up to [30] days) and then deleted.
  • Records related to abuse or blacklisting may be kept longer to stop repeat violations.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect data, such as limiting access, keeping credentials and tokens private, and using reputable hosting providers. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

You may request at any time to:

  • Access the data we hold that is linked to your Discord user ID;
  • Correct data that is wrong;
  • Delete the data we hold about you or your server.

To make a request, contact us through the support server above. We may need to confirm that you are the person (or the server owner/administrator) the data relates to before acting on it. Server administrators can also remove the Bot from their server at any time, which stops further collection for that server.

8. Children's privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in their country). Vexuo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under the minimum age has provided data to the Bot, contact us and we will delete it.
