Vexuo Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what information the Discord application "Vexuo" ("Vexuo", "the Bot", "we", "us") handles, how it is used, how long it is kept, and what choices you have. By adding or using Vexuo, you agree to the practices described here. If you do not agree, please do not use the Bot.

1. Who we are

Vexuo is a Discord bot that lets you download Roblox assets by ID. It is operated by an independent developer. You can contact us at any time through our support server: Vexuo Support Server.

2. Information we handle

Vexuo does not use a database and does not keep user profiles. The information below is only handled while the Bot is working on your request.

  • Discord command data: when you use a command such as /asset, Discord sends the Bot your user ID, username, the server and channel IDs, and the options you typed (for example, a Roblox asset ID). We use this only to respond to your command.
  • Roblox asset requests: the asset ID you submit is sent to Roblox's public services to retrieve the asset file and its public details (name, type, creator, description). The Bot then sends the result back to you in Discord. Downloaded files are processed in memory and are not saved by us after delivery.
  • Member and presence information: the Bot has access to the member list and the online/offline status of members in servers it is in. It uses this only to decide whether the Bot itself should appear online. It does not store, log, analyze, share or otherwise use any individual user's status or member information for any other purpose.
  • Temporary memory: a small in-memory cache (such as the type of an asset that was recently requested) is used to make the Bot work. It is cleared whenever the Bot restarts.
  • Technical logs: our hosting provider keeps standard logs of the Bot's activity. These may include error messages and the status of requests to Roblox (which can contain an asset ID). We do not intentionally put your name or other personal information in them.

The Bot uses slash commands and does not read the content of your normal messages. We do not collect passwords, email addresses, payment details, government ID or your location. We never ask for your Roblox account or password, and you should never share them with the Bot.

3. How we use information

  • To run the Bot and respond to your commands.
  • To detect, prevent and investigate abuse, spam and violations of our Terms of Service.
  • To fix bugs and keep the Bot stable.
  • To answer support requests and comply with legal obligations.

We do not use your data for advertising or profiling, we do not sell it, and we do not use it to train artificial intelligence models.

4. Sharing of information

We do not sell, rent or trade your information. Information is only shared in these limited cases:

  • Discord: the Bot runs on Discord, so your use of it is also governed by Discord's Privacy Policy and Terms of Service.
  • Roblox: the asset IDs you request are sent to Roblox's services in order to retrieve the assets. Roblox handles that information under its own Privacy Policy. Any Roblox authentication used by the Bot belongs to the Bot's operator, not to you.
  • Hosting provider: the Bot is hosted on third-party infrastructure (currently Render), which processes data on our behalf only to keep the Bot running.
  • Legal and safety: if required by law, a valid legal request, or to protect the rights, safety and security of users, the Bot or the public.

5. Data retention

  • Vexuo does not keep your commands, asset requests or downloaded files in a database or file storage.
  • Data handled while a command runs is discarded once the response is sent, and the in-memory cache is cleared when the Bot restarts.
  • Hosting logs are kept for a short period set by our hosting provider and are then deleted.
  • If we block a user or server for abuse, we may keep the ID of that user or server for as long as needed to enforce the block.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the Bot, such as keeping credentials and tokens private and using reputable hosting. However, no system is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

7. Your choices and rights

Because we keep very little data, there is usually nothing stored about you. You may still contact us to ask what we hold about you or your server, to correct it, or to have it deleted. To make a request, contact us through the support server above. We may need to confirm that you are the person (or the server owner/administrator) the request relates to. Server administrators can remove the Bot from their server at any time, which stops all further handling of that server's information.

8. Children's privacy

Discord requires users to be at least 13 years old (or the minimum age required in their country). Vexuo is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under the minimum age has used the Bot, contact us and we will delete any related data.

9. Third-party links and services

The Bot may display links to third-party websites (for example, Roblox pages). We are not responsible for the privacy practices of those third parties, and we encourage you to read their policies. Vexuo is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The "Last updated" date at the top shows when it last changed. If you keep using the Bot after an update, you accept the revised policy.

11. Contact

Questions or requests about this policy? Join our Vexuo Support Server.
